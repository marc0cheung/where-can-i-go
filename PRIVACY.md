# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

Where Can I Go (跟住去邊度) is built so that your travel records stay yours. This policy explains what that means.

## We don't collect your data

The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We never receive your travel records, visas, photos or any other personal information.

## What stays on your device

Your passport, trips, wishlist, visas and attachments, profile, friends' travel cards and settings are stored only in the app's own folder on your iPhone. They may be included in your iCloud or computer backups, depending on your device settings.

## When data leaves your device

Only when you choose to:

- **Export a backup.** The file goes wherever you save or send it.
- **Share a travel card or image.** It contains only what you chose in Settings → Sharing. Notes, passport, My Visas and visa attachments are never included.
- **Search the web for a country.** Your chosen search engine receives the search.
- **Open a link or send feedback.** The website or your mail app handles it under its own policy.

## Apple services

The map uses Apple Maps (MapKit), which may load map tiles from Apple. [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) applies to that. The app doesn't ask for your location.

## Deleting your data

Delete any record in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Backup files you exported are not affected.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app and published here.

## Contact

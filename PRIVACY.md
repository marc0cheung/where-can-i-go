# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

Where Can I Go (跟住去邊度) is built so that your travel records stay yours. This policy explains what that means.

## We don't collect your data

The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We never receive your travel records, visas, photos or any other personal information.

## What stays on your device

Your passport, trips, wishlist, visas and attachments, profile, friends' travel cards and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. They may be included in your iCloud or computer backups, depending on your device settings.

## When data leaves your device

Apart from the Apple services described below, data leaves the app only when you choose to:

- **Export a backup.** The file goes wherever you save or send it.
- **Share a travel card or image.** It contains only what you chose in Settings → Sharing. Notes, passport, My Visas and visa attachments are never included.
- **Search the web for a country.** Your chosen search engine receives the search.
- **Open a link or send feedback.** The website or your mail app handles it under its own policy.

## Apple services

The map uses Apple Maps (MapKit), which may load map tiles from Apple. [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) applies to that. The app doesn't ask for your location.

When you first share a travel card, the app creates a signing key and revision counter in Apple's Keychain. These identify your later cards as coming from the same sharer. If iCloud Keychain is available and enabled, Apple may sync them to your devices. This does not sync your travel records, visas or attachments, and we do not receive the key.

## Deleting your data

Delete travel, visa and friend records in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its private files from your device, but Keychain items, iCloud or computer backups, exported files and copies of cards held by recipients are not removed. The sharing identity may remain in Keychain through reinstallations. The app does not currently provide a control to reset that identity.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app and published here.

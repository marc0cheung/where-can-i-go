Bundled map border provenance

The bundled FeatureCollection is named "ne_10m_admin_0_countries".
It contains Natural Earth's 1:10m Admin-0 Countries, distributed as GeoJSON by
datasets/geo-countries:

  https://github.com/datasets/geo-countries
  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-0-countries/

Natural Earth data is public domain:
  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The GeoJSON package is offered under PDDL 1.0:
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/pddl/1.0/

Credit: Natural Earth, Lexman and the Open Knowledge Foundation.
The exact downloaded upstream revision was not recorded. Do not infer a release
version from the filename. CountryMapView and WorldMapView simplify the geometry
at runtime; no replacement dataset is downloaded by the app.

Apple Maps supplies the underlying MapKit map, not the bundled border polygons.
Credits and licence links also appear in Settings > About > Acknowledgements & Sources.

Updating the data

Download the world countries GeoJSON file and place it in this Resources folder.

URL:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/datasets/geo-countries/master/data/countries.geojson

Save as:
  Resources/world-countries.geojson

The current bundled file is about 14.6 MB and contains ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 codes
under "ISO3166-1-Alpha-3". CountryMapView also accepts legacy "ISO_A3" keys.
Check the source schema, licence and attribution when replacing the snapshot.

Quick download (macOS Terminal):
  curl -L -o world-countries.geojson \
    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/datasets/geo-countries/master/data/countries.geojson

After downloading, drag the file into your Xcode project's Resources group
(make sure "Copy items if needed" and "Add to target" are checked).
